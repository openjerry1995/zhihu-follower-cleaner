# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

**Last updated / 最后更新:** 2025-05

---

## English

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

**Data stored locally:**
- Extension settings (delay, jitter) are saved in Chrome's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`)
- Task progress (removed count, skipped users) is stored locally to support resume functionality

**What the extension does:**
- Interacts with Zhihu pages (zhihu.com) to manage your followers list
- All operations happen locally in your browser

**What the extension does NOT do:**
- Does not send any data to external servers
- Does not collect browsing history
- Does not track user behavior
- Does not use analytics or advertising SDKs

## 中文

本扩展不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据到外部服务器。

**本地存储的数据：**
- 扩展设置（延迟、抖动）保存在 Chrome 本地存储中
- 任务进度（已移除数量、已跳过用户）存储在本地以支持断点续传

**扩展功能：**
- 仅在知乎页面上操作，管理你的粉丝列表
- 所有操作均在浏览器本地完成

**扩展不会：**
- 向外部服务器发送任何数据
- 收集浏览历史
- 跟踪用户行为
- 使用分析或广告 SDK
